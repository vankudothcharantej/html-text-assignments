Event Overview

The National Career & Education Fair 2026 is a large platform where students, graduates, job seekers, educational institutions and employers can connect.

The event provides opportunities to explore jobs, internships, higher education, scholarships and career guidance.

Participants can interact with organizations, attend expert sessions, learn about different career paths and understand the skills required by today's industries.

Students and job seekers are encouraged to register early and prepare their resumes before attending.

Event Objectives

  • Connect students with employers.
  • Provide information about higher education.
  • Promote internship and apprenticeship opportunities.
  • Provide scholarship information.
  • Help participants understand industry requirements.
  • Develop career planning and professional skills.

Preparation Guide

Participants should prepare before attending the event so they can make better use of the available opportunities.

  • Prepare an updated resume.
  • Research participating organizations.
  • Carry your student or identity card.
  • Prepare a short self-introduction.
  • Prepare questions for recruiters.
  • Wear suitable professional or formal clothing.
  • Carry copies of your resume if required.

Attendee Journey

  1. Register for the event.
  2. Receive registration confirmation.
  3. Check the event schedule.
  4. Visit organization booths.
  5. Attend useful career sessions.
  6. Interact with recruiters and experts.
  7. Collect relevant information and opportunities.
  8. Follow up after the event.
