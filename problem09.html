Student Terms & Rules

General Rules

1. Students must maintain discipline and follow all institutional rules.

2. Students must carry their valid college ID card whenever they are on campus.

Attendance

3. Students must maintain the minimum required attendance percentage.

Attendance will be recorded for every class. Students should regularly attend lectures to avoid academic problems.

Examination

4. Students must follow all examination instructions given by the institution.

Students must reach the examination hall before the scheduled time. Late entry may not be permitted.

Assignment Submission

5. All assignments must be submitted before the given deadline.

Assignment Deadline: 5 September 2026
Late submissions may receive reduced marks.

Discipline

6. Students must behave respectfully with teachers, staff, and other students.

Any form of inappropriate behaviour may result in disciplinary action.

Important Notice

Students are responsible for reading and following all college rules and regulations.
