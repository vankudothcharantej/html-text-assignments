Science Notes

Chemistry

Chemistry is the study of matter and its properties.

Water: H2O

Hydrogen Peroxide: H2O2

Carbon Dioxide: CO2

Oxygen: O2

Sulfuric Acid: H2SO4

Atoms are the basic units of matter.

Physics

Physics is the study of matter, energy, motion, and forces.

Area = m2

Volume = m3

Speed = Distance / Time

Energy = mc2

The speed of light is approximately 3 × 108 m/s.

Biology

Biology is the scientific study of living organisms.

The cell is the basic structural and functional unit of life.

DNA contains genetic information required for the development and functioning of living organisms.
