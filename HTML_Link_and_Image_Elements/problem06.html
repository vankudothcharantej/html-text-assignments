TECHFEST 2026

Innovate. Create. Compete.

TechFest 2026 is a fictional college technology festival designed to bring together students interested in programming, artificial intelligence, robotics and innovation.

Introduction

The festival provides students with opportunities to participate in technical competitions, attend learning sessions and present creative technology projects. The event encourages students to learn through practical experience and teamwork.

TechFest 2026 is open to students interested in technology, innovation and problem solving.

Highlights

The festival will feature coding challenges, robotics demonstrations, AI projects, technical workshops and project presentations.

Students can use the event to meet other learners and develop practical technical skills.

Date and Venue

Date: 15 October 2026 to 17 October 2026
Venue: Main Auditorium and Innovation Centre
College Campus

Registration Deadline: 10 October 2026

Speakers

Technology professionals, faculty members and experienced student innovators will participate in the event. Speakers will discuss emerging technologies and practical career skills.
