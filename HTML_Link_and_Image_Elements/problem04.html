Agra Weekend Travel Guide

Agra is a historic city in Uttar Pradesh and is one of India's famous tourist destinations. It is known for its historic monuments, architecture, culture and traditional food.

Attractions

The Taj Mahal is one of the most famous attractions in Agra. Visitors can also explore Agra Fort and other historic sites located around the city.

Taj Mahal Reference

Culture

Agra has a rich cultural history influenced by the Mughal period. Visitors can experience traditional crafts, historical architecture and local cultural traditions.

Food

Agra is known for traditional North Indian food and famous local sweets. Visitors can try dishes such as Mughlai cuisine, paratha and the well-known Agra petha.

Best Time to Visit

October to March is generally considered a comfortable season for visiting Agra.

The cooler months are suitable for sightseeing and exploring outdoor historical attractions.

Travel Tips

Carry drinking water, comfortable footwear and keep your important belongings secure while sightseeing.

Plan monument visits according to official opening hours and check current ticket information before travelling.
