Breaking News: College Announces Technology Fest 2026

Students Prepare for a Three-Day Celebration of Technology and Innovation

A college has announced a major technology festival for students. The event will bring together students interested in programming, artificial intelligence, robotics and emerging technologies.

The festival will be held on the college campus and will include technical activities, project demonstrations, coding competitions and learning sessions. Students from different departments can participate in the event.

The three-day festival will begin on 15 October 2026. The organizers have announced several activities to encourage students to demonstrate their technical and creative skills.

The main activities will take place at the College Main Auditorium and Innovation Centre. The location will provide space for workshops, demonstrations and student projects.

The event is being organized to encourage students to explore new technologies and develop practical skills. Teamwork and innovation will be important parts of the festival.
