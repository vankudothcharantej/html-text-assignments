4. Steps Before a Practical Class

Students should follow these steps before entering the laboratory:

  1. Check the practical schedule and laboratory number.
  2. Read the practical experiment instructions.
  3. Bring the required notebook and study material.
  4. Reach the laboratory before the scheduled time.
  5. Listen to the faculty instructions.
  6. Perform the experiment according to the instructions.
  7. Save your work and submit the practical record.

6. Academic Terms

Lecture
A classroom session where theoretical concepts are explained.
Lab
A practical session where students perform experiments or programming activities.
Tutorial
A guided academic session focused on solving problems and understanding concepts.
Internal Assessment
Evaluation conducted during the semester through tests, assignments, quizzes or other academic activities.
Practical
An activity where students apply theoretical knowledge through hands-on work.

7. Academic Rules

  • Students must maintain regular attendance.
  • Students must carry their college ID card.
  • Students must arrive on time for lectures and practicals.
  • Mobile phones should be used only when permitted by the faculty.
  • Students must maintain cleanliness in classrooms and laboratories.
  • Students must submit assignments before the specified deadline.
  • Students must follow all laboratory safety instructions.
