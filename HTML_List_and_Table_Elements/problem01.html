1. Admission Overview

GLA University offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs in engineering, management, science, computer applications and other disciplines.

Students should carefully check the eligibility requirements, complete the application process and submit all required documents before the deadline.

Mandatory requirement: Applicants must provide accurate academic and personal information.

2. Eligibility

Candidates must satisfy the academic qualifications specified for their selected program.

  • Passed the required qualifying examination.
  • Meet the minimum percentage requirement.
  • Meet program-specific subject requirements.
  • Submit valid academic documents.

Application Deadline: 30 June 2027

3. Application Process

Follow these steps in the given sequence:

  1. Visit the official university admission website.
  2. Create a new registration account.
  3. Fill in the online application form.
  4. Upload the required documents.
  5. Pay the application fee.
  6. Submit the completed application form.
  7. Attend counselling or selection procedures if required.
  8. Complete admission and registration formalities.

4. Required Documents

Applicants should keep the following documents ready:

  • Class 10 Mark Sheet
  • Class 12 Mark Sheet
  • Transfer Certificate
  • Migration Certificate
  • Passport-size Photographs
  • Government-issued Identity Proof
  • Category Certificate, if applicable

5. Important Terms

Eligibility
The academic and other requirements that a student must satisfy to apply for a program.
Merit List
A list of candidates prepared according to their academic performance or admission criteria.
Counselling
A process where eligible students receive guidance and may select their preferred course or seat.
Scholarship
Financial assistance provided to eligible students according to specified criteria.
Registration
The process of officially enrolling a student in the university.
