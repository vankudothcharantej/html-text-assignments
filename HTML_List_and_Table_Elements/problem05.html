1. Fest Introduction

Annual College Fest 2026 is a three-day inter-college celebration featuring technical competitions, cultural performances, sports, quizzes, coding challenges and creative activities.

Students can participate individually or as teams depending on the competition. Participants should register before the registration deadline and follow the event rules.

4. Registration Process

Follow the steps below to register for a competition:

  1. Choose your preferred competition.
  2. Check the eligibility requirements.
  3. Click the registration link.
  4. Enter your personal and college details.
  5. Select individual or team participation.
  6. Pay the registration fee, if applicable.
  7. Submit the registration form.
  8. Save your registration/reference number.
  9. Report to the venue at the scheduled time.

5. General Fest Rules

  • Participants must carry a valid college ID card.
  • Participants must report to the venue before the event begins.
  • Participants must follow instructions given by event coordinators.
  • Any form of cheating or unfair practice may lead to disqualification.
  • Participants must maintain discipline and cleanliness.
  • Participants are responsible for their personal belongings.
  • Teams must not exceed the maximum team size specified for their competition.
  • The decision of the judging panel will be recorded as the official result of the competition.

6. Important Fest Terms

Open Event
An event in which participants meeting the announced eligibility requirements can participate.
Team Event
A competition in which two or more participants compete together as a team.
Individual Event
A competition in which one participant competes individually.
Registration
The process of officially entering a participant or team into an event.
Coordinator
A person responsible for managing a particular event or category.
Final Round
The concluding stage in which selected participants compete for the final positions.
