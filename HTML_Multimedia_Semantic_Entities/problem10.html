Welcome to National Tech & Career Expo 2026

India's Technology & Career Networking Event

The National Tech & Career Expo brings together students, technology companies, recruiters, educators, entrepreneurs and industry experts on one platform.

Participants can explore internships, placements, workshops, certifications and career opportunities.

The event focuses on emerging technologies such as Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Cloud Computing, Cybersecurity, Data Science and Web Development.

About the Expo

Event Introduction

The National Tech & Career Expo is designed to help students understand the changing technology industry and prepare for future careers.

Visitors can interact with organizations, attend technical sessions, participate in workshops and learn directly from experienced professionals.

Organizer Message

Our goal is to connect education with real-world industry opportunities.

"The future belongs to learners who continuously develop their skills and remain curious about new technology."

— National Tech & Career Expo Organizing Committee

Career Expo Terminology

Career Expo
An event where students and professionals interact with organizations and career experts.
Internship
A temporary learning and work opportunity that provides practical industry experience.
Placement
A recruitment opportunity where an organization hires eligible students or professionals.
Workshop
An interactive session focused on developing a particular skill.
Certification
A credential that demonstrates completion of a course, training program or assessment.
Networking
Building professional relationships with people in an industry.
