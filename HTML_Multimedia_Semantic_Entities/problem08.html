Financial Education Overview

Understanding Personal Finance

Personal finance means managing your income, expenses, savings, borrowing and investments responsibly. Good financial planning can help you prepare for future goals.

A bank may provide services such as savings accounts, current accounts, debit cards, credit cards and loans.

Example: If your monthly income is ₹30,000 and your expenses are ₹20,000, you can plan the remaining amount for savings and other financial goals.

"A good financial plan starts with understanding where your money goes."

⚠ Important Notice

This portal is for educational purposes only. Interest rates, charges, eligibility conditions and product features may change. Always check the official financial institution's current terms before making a financial decision.

Bank Accounts

Savings Account

A savings account is generally used to keep money safely while earning interest according to the bank's applicable rate.

  • Useful for regular savings
  • Provides access to banking services
  • May offer interest on eligible balances
  • Can be connected to a debit card
  • Online and mobile banking may be available

Current Account

Current accounts are generally designed for frequent transactions, especially for businesses and organizations.

Account Opening Steps

  1. Choose a suitable account.
  2. Check eligibility and required documents.
  3. Complete the application form.
  4. Complete identity verification/KYC requirements.
  5. Submit the required documents.
  6. Receive account details after approval.

Bank Cards

Debit Card

A debit card normally allows you to make purchases or withdraw money using funds available in your linked bank account.

Credit Card

A credit card provides a credit facility subject to the issuer's terms. Borrowed amounts may attract interest or other charges if not paid according to the applicable terms.

Tip: Understand the billing cycle, payment due date, interest and applicable fees before using a credit card.

Loans & Borrowing

What is a Loan?

A loan is money borrowed from a financial institution or lender that is generally repaid over an agreed period, usually with interest and possible additional charges.

Common examples include education loans, vehicle loans, home loans and personal loans.

Responsible Borrowing

  • Borrow only what you can reasonably repay.
  • Compare applicable interest rates and charges.
  • Understand the repayment schedule.
  • Check processing and other applicable fees.
  • Read the loan agreement carefully.

Important Financial Terms

Interest Rate
The percentage used to calculate interest charged on borrowing or paid on certain deposits.
EMI
Equated Monthly Instalment — a scheduled payment made toward certain loans.
Principal
The original amount of money borrowed or deposited, depending on the financial product.
Tenure
The agreed period over which a financial product or loan remains active or is repaid.

Responsible Personal Finance Tips

  • Create a monthly budget.
  • Track your regular expenses.
  • Build an emergency savings fund.
  • Avoid unnecessary borrowing.
  • Pay bills and loan instalments on time.
  • Understand fees before using financial products.
  • Protect your banking passwords and OTPs.
  • Never share confidential banking information with strangers.
  • Review your account statements regularly.
  • Set realistic financial goals.

Remember: small, consistent financial habits can make a difference over time.
