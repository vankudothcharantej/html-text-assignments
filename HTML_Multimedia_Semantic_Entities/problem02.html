Recruitment Process

Hiring Stages

  1. Submit your application
  2. Resume screening
  3. Technical assessment
  4. Technical interview
  5. HR interview
  6. Offer and joining process

Important Recruitment Terms

Screening
Initial review of a candidate's resume, qualifications and application details.
Technical Round
An assessment of technical knowledge, programming, problem-solving or role-specific skills.
HR Round
A discussion about communication, career goals, workplace expectations and other relevant topics.
Offer
A formal employment proposal containing applicable job and employment terms.
